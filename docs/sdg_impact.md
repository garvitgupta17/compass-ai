# Compass AI - UN SDG 4 Alignment & Social Impact

## 1. Context & Mission
Compass AI was developed as a flagship project for the **1M1B Virtual Internship "AI for Sustainability"**.
The initiative directly addresses **UN Sustainable Development Goal 4: Quality Education** by democratizing personalized career guidance for students and aspiring engineers worldwide.

---

## 2. Core Pillars of SDG 4 Alignment

### 🎯 Target 4.4: Relevant Skills for Employment
- **Problem**: Aspiring engineers face overwhelming information overload and contradictory learning advice.
- **Compass AI Solution**: Provides a deterministic, transparent skill-gap analysis and capacity-constrained weekly study roadmap targeting high-demand industry roles (`Data Analyst`, `Data Engineer`, `ML Engineer`).

### 🌍 Target 4.5: Equal Access & Inclusivity
- **Problem**: High-quality tech mentorship is often locked behind expensive bootcamps and subscription paywalls.
- **Compass AI Solution**:
  - **Budget Accessibility**: Features strict hard-constraint filtering for **100% Free** learning resources.
  - **Language Inclusivity**: Includes top-tier regional language support (such as **Hindi** courses) to empower diverse student communities.

### 💡 Transparency & AI Ethics
- **Problem**: Black-box AI recommendations can mislead students with inaccurate timelines or hallucinated courses.
- **Compass AI Solution**:
  - **Deterministic Rules Engine**: Core gap scoring, priorities, and roadmaps are calculated using open, mathematical formulas.
  - **Zero-Hallucination RAG**: All recommended learning links stem strictly from curated, verified databases.

---

## 3. Scalability & Future Sustainability
Compass AI's modular architecture allows effortless scaling to add new career domains (e.g. Web Development, Cybersecurity, Cloud DevOps) and expand multi-lingual support globally.
